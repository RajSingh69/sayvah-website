Need a hand? Someone nearby wants to help.

Who it's for
Anyone. SayVah grew out of the Sikh idea of seva, but you don't need to be Sikh, or know any of the words, to use it.

How it works, from both sides.

If you need help

  1. Download SayVah and set up your profile.
  2. Post a request. Say what you need, roughly where, and when.
  3. Wait for offers from local sevadaars who can do it.
  4. Look at their profile and follow the approval steps before you meet anyone.
  5. Sort out the details in the app's chat.
  6. Mark it done once you've been helped.

If you want to volunteer

  1. Make your profile useful: a clear photo, your area and a bit about you.
  2. Browse requests near you.
  3. Offer to help with one you really have the time and skills for.
  4. Wait for approval. Don't turn up until the app says you're connected.
  5. Agree the time, place and task in the chat.
  6. Help, then mark it complete.

This is what the app looks like.

Helping strangers should feel safe. Here's how SayVah handles it.

  • An admin approves connections where the request needs it, before anyone goes ahead.
  • You can see who someone is from their profile before you accept or offer help.
  • You can report anyone who behaves badly, and the team will look at it.
  • You can block anyone you don't want to hear from again.
  • Chat stays in the app, so arrangements are clear and can be checked if something goes wrong.
  • Gurdwaras and organisations can be involved, adding people who know the community.

SayVah is not an emergency service.

If someone is in danger or needs urgent professional help, call the emergency services. Never share passwords, bank details or anything sensitive in a request.

    What happens to your information.

    What SayVah collects, why, where it's kept, who can see it, and what you can do about it. The full notice is on the Privacy page.

    SayVah processes personal information to provide the service, connect requesters with Sevadaars, operate community safety features, administer accounts and support participating areas and organisations.

    This section explains the main categories of information used by SayVah, how that information is used and the controls available to users.

    Clear categories

    SayVah explains the main account, activity, communication, safety, location and website information used to operate the service.

    No unsupported claims

    SayVah does not describe data as time-limited, UK-only, end-to-end encrypted or automatically deleted unless that is confirmed by the implemented service.

    Other SayVah users may see

    • Public profile information needed for community interaction.
    • Name and profile image where these are displayed in the app.
    • Role, area and verification or trust indicators where applicable.
    • Information intentionally included in a community request.

    Not intended for public display

    • Account email address.
    • Private contact details and internal account administration fields.
    • Reports, moderation notes and safety review information.
    • Internal verification review information and evidence fields.

    Location & Live Seva

    SayVah includes location-related functionality associated with active Seva and safety features. Location information is not intended to be displayed publicly to the wider SayVah community.

    Active areas
    SayVah uses area and location records to show active SayVah service areas.
    Location sessions
    SayVah includes location-session records for location-enabled features. The exact app trigger for location permission must be confirmed from the live app before SayVah describes a specific permission moment publicly.
    Administrator access
    Authorised SayVah administrators may access location-related records where required to operate the service, support active Seva or investigate safety issues.
    Retention
    SayVah does not currently publish a fixed location-data retention period. This must be specified in the full Privacy Notice before location retention is represented as time-limited.

    Are my messages private?

    Messages are stored as part of the SayVah service and are intended for the participants involved in the relevant conversation or request. Authorised SayVah administrators may access relevant conversations when necessary for platform administration, moderation, safeguarding, investigating reports or resolving disputes. SayVah does not describe messages as end-to-end encrypted.

    How does verification work?

    Verification decisions and review status are recorded against the user's SayVah account. Authorised administrators can review and approve or deny verification. Verification records may include whether the account is verified, the verification status, approval status, review date, reviewing administrator and review message. SayVah does not currently operate a general ID-document upload system unless an identity document is specifically requested through an implemented verification process.

    SayVah does not sell users' personal information.SayVah does not currently use users' personal information to operate third-party advertising within the service.
